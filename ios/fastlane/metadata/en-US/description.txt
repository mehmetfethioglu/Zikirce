Hasibu is an offline-first enterprise time and attendance (PDKS), shift scheduling, leave tracking, and encrypted personnel dossier platform built for multi-branch companies, factories, and retail teams.

Unlike cloud-locked HR platforms, Hasibu keeps your employee and attendance records locally encrypted with SQLCipher AES-256 on your iPhone or iPad and lets you synchronize client-side AES-256-GCM encrypted backups directly to your own Google Drive account.

KEY CAPABILITIES

• Live Attendance Dashboard & Multi-Branch Isolation
Monitor real-time clock-ins, late arrivals, early departures, and absences across every branch from one unified cockpit. Multi-branch scoping and strict supervisor isolation ensure each manager only accesses authorized branches.

• Biometric Face Terminal Sync & Branch Bridge
Pull attendance logs over TCP/IP from Tenfei D35 and ZKTeco-compatible terminals. Connect branch bridge agents for conflict-free per-branch encrypted inbox synchronization.

• Dynamic QR / GPS Branch Check-In
Verify on-site check-ins for field and store staff using 30-second rotating TOTP (HMAC-SHA1) QR codes combined with GPS branch coordinate validation.

• Multi-Day Attendance Reports & CSV Export
Filter attendance, lateness, overtime, and absence summaries by date range, branch, and employment status, then export clean CSV reports in one tap.

• Leave Management, Corrections & Employee Self-Service
Record annual leave, sick reports, and excused absences using a searchable employee directory and quick-day shortcuts. Staff can submit HR requests and view shift schedules through the Employee Portal.

• Encrypted Digital Personnel Dossier
Track mandatory and optional onboarding documents with missing/uploaded filters and store files in an AES-256-GCM encrypted local vault.

• Unified Auto Sign-In & Password Recovery
Automatically detects admin, branch supervisor, and employee accounts without switching tabs, enforces a first-setup admin password shield, and supports 6-digit one-time verification code recovery via your company Gmail.

PRIVACY & ZERO-TRACKING ARCHITECTURE
Hasibu contains no ads, runs zero third-party analytics trackers, and uses the restricted Google Drive drive.file scope solely for encrypted backups created by Hasibu.
