Tahrir is a 100% offline-first smart PDF, EPUB, and Markdown reader designed to turn scanned, multi-column, and poorly formatted documents into an effortless reading experience on iPhone and iPad.

Smart PDF Reflow & Multi-Column Academic Layout
• Automatically detect two-column academic articles and reflow text in true left-to-right reading order with preserved headings and paragraphs.
• Switch seamlessly between Warm Parchment, Ink Night, and true AMOLED Black themes, one-tap Auto-Crop margin zoom, and direction-aware page alignment.
• Tap inline footnote markers to inspect references cleanly without losing your place.

300 DPI On-Device OCR & Morphological Proofreading
• Scan image-based or corrupted-OCR PDF pages locally at 300 DPI and cache cleaned pages in SQLite for instant reopening.
• Repair OCR ligature fusions, consonant devoicing typos, broken hyphens, and contextual confusions using a 34,795-root morphological lexicon and Word-style Proofread & Full-Book Scan panel.

One-Tap Popup Lexicon & Compound Analysis
• Tap any word directly inside your book to inspect definitions, etymology, stem+suffix morphology, and one-tap OCR corrections without leaving the page.

Guided Flow Reading Coach & Eye Workshop
• Start with a 60-second lexical warmup generated from your active book, then flow through pages with 2–4 word semantic chunk highlighting and gradual scaffolding fade.
• Track zero-penalty daily streaks, density-adaptive target WPM, Original PDF line-ruler pacing, active recall reflections, and optional Eye Workshop drills (Pyramid, Tachistoscope, Schulte Table).

Marginalia, Obsidian Export, QR Exchange & Quote Studio
• Annotate passages across 4 categories and 5 ink colors, export clean Obsidian/Notion Markdown (.md) notes, or exchange marginalia offline via peer-to-peer QR Code.
• Create high-resolution PNG quote cards in Parchment, 9:16 Story, Square, or AMOLED styles and unlock sealed Book Completion Certificates.

Offline TTS Karaoke, Procedural Soundscapes & Zero Tracking
• Listen with live karaoke word tracking and procedural offline ambient audio (Rain, Fireplace, Library, Café, Ney) with automatic TTS audio ducking.
• Full JSON library backup & restore, Files app integration, zero ads, and zero third-party telemetry.
