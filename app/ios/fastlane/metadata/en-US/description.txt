Zamera is a 100% on-device professional camera, true-color calibration studio, and cinema video app built for artists, photographers, and mobile filmmakers.

FOUR STUDIO MODES IN ONE UNIFIED APP:

1. ART MODE — TRUE COLOR (ΔE₀₀ ≤ 1.0)
• 24-Patch ColorChecker Calibration: Solves persistent per-lens 3x3 CCM matrices and 8x6 flat-field corrections directly from a 14-bit RAW frame of an X-Rite / Calibrite ColorChecker Classic target.
• Scientific CIEDE2000 Engine: Embedded Rust core (zamera_core) executes Sharma 2005 CIEDE2000 math, Bradford D50→D65 adaptation, and constant-hue gamut compression without vendor tone-curve distortion.
• Live Colorimeter & A/B Split Curtain: Inspect real-time CIE L*a*b* (D50), #HEX, and ΔE₀₀ readings, or compare TrueColor 1:1 against stock OEM rendering side-by-side.

2. PRO PHOTO & MANUAL DSLR (PASM)
• 14-Bit Bayer RAW & Apple ProRAW (.DNG): Discrete physical lens lock (13mm ultrawide, 24mm main, 100mm telephoto), 1/3-EV stops for ISO, shutter speed, Kelvin/Tint white balance, AEL/AFL lock, and ±2 EV 3-frame AE bracketing.
• Live Light Meter & 64-Bin RGB Histogram: Monitor highlights, shadows, and channel balance in real time and after capture.

3. STUDIO PORTRAIT & 100% TRANSPARENT PNG STICKERS
• Virtual Aperture & Studio Lighting: Re-adjust f/1.4–f/16 computational bokeh and studio light intensity post-capture.
• Apple Vision Sticker Studio: Cut tapped subjects into tight-cropped, edge-decontaminated 100% transparent or white-outlined PNG stickers and copy directly to the clipboard with full UTType.png alpha.

4. PRO CINEMA VIDEO & MEDIA STUDIO
• 180° Shutter Lock, Apple Log & ProRes 422 HQ: Capture 4K/1080p 24/25/30/60p with custom .cube 3D LUT preview, Rack Focus (A·B·A↔B), False Color IRE, and 48kHz stereo dBFS audio meters.
• Pro Media Studio: Review clips in an SMPTE timecode cinema player, extract still frames, cut stickers from frames, filter across 6 archive categories, and share JPEG or DNG RAW files separately.
